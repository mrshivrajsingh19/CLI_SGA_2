QUESTION 2 - EXPLANATIONS

1. fork()
The fork() system call creates child processes from the parent process. Each child can execute independently.

2. Process monitoring
The parent process monitors the execution of child processes using their process IDs and waits for their completion.

3. waitpid()
waitpid() allows the parent to wait for specific child processes and collect their exit status.

4. Zombie prevention
The parent calls waitpid() to reap terminated child processes, preventing them from remaining as zombie processes.

5. Signal handling
Signals provide a mechanism for communicating with and controlling processes.

6. SIGTERM and SIGKILL
SIGTERM requests graceful termination of an unresponsive child. If the child does not respond, SIGKILL can force termination.

7. Overall solution
fork() creates child processes, monitoring checks their execution, waitpid() cleans up terminated children, and signals allow unresponsive children to be terminated.
