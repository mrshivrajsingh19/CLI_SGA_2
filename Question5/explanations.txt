QUESTION 5 - EXPLANATIONS

This question evaluates theoretical recovery mechanisms available in vi.

Swap files and auto-recovery are the most useful mechanisms after an unexpected crash because they can preserve and recover unsaved editing changes.

Undo history and registers are useful during an active editing session but are less suitable as the primary crash-recovery method.

Backup files provide an additional safety layer by preserving the previous version of the configuration file.

The recommended strategy is to recover from the swap file after a crash, verify the recovered configuration carefully, and maintain backup files as an additional safeguard.
