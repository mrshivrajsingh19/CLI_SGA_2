QUESTION 5 - EXPLANATIONS

This question is theoretical and does not require execution of Linux commands.

The answer evaluates the recovery mechanisms available in vi, including swap files, undo history, registers, backup files, and auto-recovery.

The most reliable strategy is to use the swap file and vi's recovery mechanism because they can recover unsaved changes after a system crash. Backup files provide an additional safeguard by preserving the previous version of the configuration file.
